How a track is made

From brief to release.

  1. 01

    Cultural Research

    Genre reference, linguistic nuance, audience mapping, and instrumentation palette selection per regional identity.

  2. 02

    Neural Audio Generation

    Melody, rhythm, arrangement, and production texture built through advanced AI audio engines guided by human direction.

  3. 03

    Human Editorial Review

    Quality control, cultural accuracy checks, lyrical alignment, and mix assessment before any track advances.

  4. 04

    Master & Distribute

    Release-ready masters uploaded to the storefront and prepared for global streaming distribution.
